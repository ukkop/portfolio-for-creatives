The Road
Back Home

Leaving home is never easy, but what happens when you are taken away from home without choice or reason? This is the reality for many Indigenous children every day. Follow the story of Ts̓xwixw, a Secwépemc child who had lived with their Kye7e for their entire 11 years of life.


When their world gets suddenly turned upside down by being taken away from their Kye7e into a foster home far away, they must learn to navigate through discovering their identity away from their community, withstand the harsh reality of the world, and find the road back home.
