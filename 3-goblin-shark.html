Goblin-Shark Surf-Guy Character Design

“Try and out-skate me, suckahs!”

Meet my Goblin-Shark skater/surfboarder: an infamous,cutthroat, competitor who uses ruthless tactics to keep his spot in the big leagues,he is terrified of losing his spot as a legend in both skating/surfing competitive scenes located in the famously vibrant and gregarious Venice Beach, where he will always find methods of cheating his way str8 to the top.

Based on an ink blotch exercise, this goblin character evolved into a shark-y hybrid while inspired by his environment and types of sports that would suit his antics and hobbies. Turning out to be both surfing and skating, whatever he can get his little goblin-shark hands on.
