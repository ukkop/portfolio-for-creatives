  • About Andy

    Andy Ukko Poystila is a Salt Spring Island sprouted man, currently residing in Burnaby BC, Canada. and happens to be an Illustrator and UI/UX Designer.

    Most of his current work is within publishing at the school newspaper, the Capilano Courier, SAD Magazine,Game Jams with his team: Game Strike Team, and other game design projects. He currently aims to complete his html-based visual novel, finish some team projects, anndddd finish twenty to thirty other personal projects.

    You can find his process work on his social media! If he is not found drawing in a cafe for 8-12 hours, then there is not really anywhere else you can find him. But one thing for sure is that soon as his degree is over, his mobile phone will be disconnected and melted with thermite. He shall burn all of his identification, money, and papers. Then will commence his sail eastward, into the rising sun.
