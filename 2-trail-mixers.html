Trail Mixers is a fictional food-truck strategy game. With help & mentorship from Kimberly Parker.

This key art work-flow introduced me to the importance of flexible asset files for promotional material & introduced a render style.

I took inspiration from my travels to the UK and Scotland, inspired by medieval folklore and literature. Trail Mixers is about a quartet of goblins whose goal is to revive the ancient ancestral food-truck art that is being lost in their land. so they are going on a quest to feed all the pilgrims, kingdoms and monasteries in the land.

Sketches for layout. aiming to focus on three key landscape beats.

I laid out the scene in blender in order to adjust the elements and simulate lighting.

This sequence represents a lighting and value technique that I learned which uses curves in order to make rendering quicker and awesome.

These mockups show how the key art's layers can be adjusted around to fit new dimensions and create new compositions for marketing and promotion of different social platforms.
