Trail Mixers
Key Art

Trail Mixers is a game that follows a group of goblins reviving an old ancestral tradition of trail mix food-trucking. For this project, I embodied my personal art style and favourite themes of fantasy and humour. This project introduced me to the importance of flexible art files and key art processes. With Mentoring with help from Kimberly Parker. Tackling the challenge of un-static layer separation.
