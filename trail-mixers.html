Trail Mixers | Key art and UI Concept

With mentoring help from the talented Kim Parker, I designed a key art piece from a home cooked game concept titled Trail Mixers, a time management narrative game that follows a group of goblins reviving an old ancestral tradition of trail mix food trucking. This project embodies my personal art style and favourite themes of fantasy and humour, and introduced me to the importance of flexible art files and key art processes.
